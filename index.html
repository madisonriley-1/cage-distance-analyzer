Scoring methodology & limitations

Country-level evidence is displayed under each CAGE dimension. The 1–5 judgments capture cross-border friction, not market attractiveness or probability of failure. The tool does not fetch live data and does not independently confirm current tariffs, sanctions, or local legal requirements. Values and developments were reviewed for this prototype in November 2026.

For country-pair comparisons, scores are symmetric but the recommended actions are written from the selected home country's perspective. Country-level GDP per capita is an imperfect proxy for customer purchasing power; apparel product-market fit should be tested separately.

Built as a transparent, self-contained educational decision-support tool inspired by Pankaj Ghemawat's CAGE distance framework. All industry weights, dimension scores and thresholds are author-assigned judgments; the underlying sources do not endorse them.
